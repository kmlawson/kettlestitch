# Validation

[Validation.md](Validation.md) records what was measured and on what, with its conclusions. The text files beside it are the raw logs those figures came from: benchmark runs on synthetic and real collections, the unit test run, UI smoke runs and the evaluation of the proposal limits.

They describe the app as it was on the dates they give, mostly 10 October 2026, and are kept as a record rather than kept current.
